About Arara

Arara lets you dictate in Brazilian Portuguese without sending any audio or text to the cloud. Press a global shortcut, speak, and the transcribed text is inserted at the cursor position in any window. The app runs the Whisper model locally, so your voice never leaves your computer.

Arara is designed for anyone who wants to type by speaking, especially for writing prompts for AI assistants, emails, messages, or code. It supports three Whisper models (base, small, large‑v3‑turbo) that are downloaded the first time you run the program. GPU acceleration via Vulkan is optional. The interface shows a live preview of the transcription and lets you choose from five Brazilian‑parrot themed visual styles. A built‑in dictionary helps correct names, acronyms, and technical terms. All settings, history, and downloaded models are stored locally in the user’s AppData folder. The app checks for updates online and can install them with a single click, but this can be disabled in the settings.

Key features

  • Global shortcut (default Ctrl + Shift + Space) to start and stop dictation
  • Live preview of transcribed text in a pop‑up window
  • Inserts the full transcribed text at the current cursor location
  • Three offline Whisper models with optional GPU acceleration
  • Custom dictionary for correcting specific words or acronyms
  • History of dictations saved locally
  • Five visual themes with light and dark modes
  • Automatic update check with one‑click install

Frequently asked questions

Is Arara free?

Yes, it is completely free and open source under the MIT license.

Does Arara send my voice or text to the internet?

No, all transcription happens locally on your computer.

How do I update Arara?

The app checks for new versions online and offers a one‑click update; you can disable this in Settings.

How do I uninstall Arara?

Use Windows’ standard Add or Remove Programs feature.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No account or subscription is required.
