About WaveFlow

WaveFlow lets you manage a personal music library on your computer without any subscription or online account. It scans folders, builds a library with album, artist and genre information, and plays files through a real‑time audio engine that supports Hi‑Res and gapless playback. The player also shows synchronized lyrics, supports plugins, and offers several visual skins.

WaveFlow is a desktop music player for Windows, macOS and Linux. It reads your music collection, enriches metadata with tempo, key and loudness information, and can fetch missing tags from MusicBrainz. Playback supports high‑resolution audio, DSD over DoP, and multi‑channel output. The AutoMix feature cross‑fades tracks beat‑matched. Lyrics are displayed word‑by‑word with optional romanisation and translations, useful for karaoke. The interface includes six skins, animated album covers, and a canvas visualizer. Additional features include smart playlists, daily mixes, podcast support, WebDAV/SMB shares, DLNA and MPD servers, and statistics. Plugins can be installed from an integrated store, extending functionality while keeping the core app sandboxed.

Key features

  • Scans folders and builds a library organised by album, artist and genre
  • Hi‑Res, gapless and bit‑perfect audio playback with DSD over DoP support
  • AutoMix cross‑fade that aligns beats between tracks
  • Word‑synced lyrics with romanisation and translations
  • Smart playlists, daily mixes and podcast integration
  • Six customizable skins with animated covers and canvas visualizer
  • Tag workshop with undo support and automatic metadata enrichment
  • Support for WebDAV, SMB shares, DLNA and MPD servers
  • Plugin system with one‑click installation from a curated store

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection to use WaveFlow?

No, WaveFlow works offline with the music files stored on your computer.

Can I import playlists from other players?

Yes, you can import common playlist formats such as M3U.

Is there a portable version?

The official releases provide installer packages; a portable ZIP is not currently offered.

How are lyrics obtained?

Lyrics are fetched from online sources when available, but they are displayed locally during playback.

Can I use WaveFlow with external DACs?

Yes, the audio engine is designed to work with high‑quality DACs and supports bit‑perfect output.
