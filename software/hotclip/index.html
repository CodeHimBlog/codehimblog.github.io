About HotClip

HotClip lets you load a video file, let the AI find highlight moments, automatically cut the selected parts into 9:16 short clips, add subtitles and export ready‑to‑post files. All processing happens on your computer, so no uploads or watermarks are added.

The program analyses the audio and visual content of a video to locate high‑interest moments, then creates short vertical clips that match the chosen length (10‑120 seconds). You can set the number of clips, choose a genre such as shopping or gaming, and let the AI generate subtitles, translate them, and even suggest short copy for social media. The interface separates source media, preview, timeline, and export controls, and supports both light and dark themes. It works with local speech‑to‑text models, speaker identification for multi‑person videos, and offers fine‑grained search of the transcript to pick exact moments. Export options include video files with embedded subtitles or separate SRT files. The tool runs entirely offline, does not require registration, and does not add watermarks.

Key features

  • AI automatically finds highlight moments and suggests clip lengths.
  • Converts landscape video to vertical 9:16 format for short‑form platforms.
  • Generates and embeds subtitles, with optional translation.
  • Allows you to set the number of clips and target duration per clip.
  • Provides a searchable transcript to pick exact sections.
  • Supports speaker identification for multi‑person recordings.
  • Offers light and dark UI themes and keyboard shortcuts.
  • Exports ready‑to‑post video files or separate subtitle files.
