About Skrin

Skrin provides a Netflix‑style interface for the video files you already own. It scans your disks or network shares, shows posters and details, and plays the media with high‑quality video and audio support.

Skrin is designed for users who keep their movies and TV shows as files on local storage, external drives, or network shares. The program does not run a server, does not modify your media files, and stores no data online unless you choose to connect services like SIMKL or Trakt. It uses mpv for playback on Windows and Linux, and the native Android player on Android TV. The interface shows posters, backdrops, cast information, and badges that indicate resolution, HDR, Dolby Vision, audio format, and aspect ratio. It remembers your progress, can skip intros and credits using community databases, and can fetch subtitles from OpenSubtitles. All settings are local, and the library is kept on your device, making it suitable for offline use.

The player supports 4K HDR, Dolby Vision, and Atmos/DTS:X audio passthrough, and it can query your display and audio equipment to select the best output. It provides detailed playback statistics, such as refresh rate and scaler information, when you press the "i" key. Skrin does not perform any upscaling, frame interpolation, or quality presets; it presents the content exactly as encoded.

Installation is straightforward via an installer or a portable ZIP, and the program can be uninstalled from the system settings without leaving residual files. Updates are manual and optional, ensuring you stay in control of changes.

Overall, Skrin offers a simple, privacy‑focused way to browse and watch your personal media collection with a modern, TV‑friendly UI.

Key features

  • Organizes local movies and TV shows with posters, backdrops, and metadata
  • Plays 4K HDR, Dolby Vision, and Atmos/DTS:X audio without transcoding
  • Queries display and audio equipment to match refresh rate and sound format
  • Remembers watch progress and can skip intros, recaps, and credits
  • Fetches subtitles automatically and can search OpenSubtitles
  • Works with NAS, USB drives, and local disks without moving or renaming files
  • No server or background services; everything runs locally
  • Optional integration with SIMKL and Trakt for scrobbling
  • Portable ZIP version available for use without installation
  • Detailed playback statistics available with a single key press

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection to use Skrin?

No, all media files are accessed locally and the library works offline; an internet connection is only needed for optional subtitle fetching or service integration.

Will Skrin modify my video files?

No, Skrin never renames, moves, or writes to your media files unless you explicitly export an .nfo file.

Can I install Skrin on a shared computer without admin rights?

Yes, the installer works for a single user account and does not require administrator privileges.

How does Skrin handle network shares?

It can read files from NAS or other network shares without moving them, and it shows how much of a file is buffered on the seek bar.

Is there a way to update Skrin automatically?

Updates are manual; you can trigger a download from Settings → Advanced, but nothing is downloaded or installed without your confirmation.
