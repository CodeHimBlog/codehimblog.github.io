About ClipMint

ClipMint lets you create short, vertical videos with automatic captioning and suggested titles, hashtags and rankings. It works locally, so no subscription or watermark is added, and you decide when to upload the result to YouTube.

The software analyses the content of a video, detects the most interesting moments, trims the clip to a 9:16 aspect ratio, adds word‑by‑word captions using a local Whisper model, removes dead air and generates a title, description and hashtags. You can review why each clip was chosen, edit the output and optionally upload directly to your YouTube channel. ClipMint runs on Windows and does not require any additional installations such as Python or FFmpeg; just double‑click the executable to start.

Key features

  • Automatically finds highlight moments in long videos or streams
  • Cuts clips to vertical 9:16 format suitable for Shorts, Reels and TikTok
  • Generates word‑by‑word captions using local speech‑to‑text
  • Removes dead air and adds suggested titles, descriptions and hashtags
  • Shows why each clip was ranked and lets you edit before saving
  • Optional one‑click upload of the finished clip to YouTube
  • Works entirely offline; no subscription or watermark

Yes, it is completely free and open source.

The program runs locally and does not require an internet connection except when you choose to upload a clip, so it is safe to use.
