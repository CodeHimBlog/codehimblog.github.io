About Astraeus

Astraeus adds a thin strip to the top (or side) of your screen. When you hover the mouse or press Win+Alt+A, a panel expands with a media player, quick toggles, system stats and other tools. It disappears when not in use, using almost no resources.

Astraeus is a lightweight, native Windows utility written in C++20 using Direct2D and DirectComposition. It does not require any runtime, installer or web view. The panel can appear from the top edge by default, or be moved to the left or right side. It hides automatically when you move the cursor away, when a fullscreen app runs, or when you press the hotkey again. The utility includes a media player with album art, visualizer, fullscreen lyrics, quick toggles for Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, volume, brightness and more. Themes adapt to dark, light, glass or custom colors, and can even pull palettes from your wallpaper or album art. The program is designed to stay idle when closed, returning GPU usage to the system within seconds.

Key features

  • Media player with album art, visualizer and fullscreen lyrics.
  • Quick toggles for Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, volume, brightness and other system controls.
  • Small pill‑style widget that shows current media while other apps are fullscreen.
  • Multiple color themes, including automatic palette extraction from wallpaper.
  • Very low resource usage; no rendering or GPU usage when the panel is hidden.
  • Hotkey (Win+Alt+A) to open or close the dashboard.
  • Supports multiple monitors with optional spread of fullscreen player.
  • Built as a single native executable, no installer or additional runtime required.

Frequently asked questions

How do I open the dashboard?

Hover the mouse at the top edge of the screen (or left/right if you moved it) or press Win+Alt+A.

Does Astraeus need to be installed?

No, it runs directly from the downloaded executable.

Will it affect my computer's performance?

It uses almost no resources when closed and releases the GPU shortly after hiding.

Can I change the hotkey?

Yes, the hotkey can be customized in the Settings menu.

Is the program safe to run?

It is open‑source under GPL‑3.0 and does not require any additional runtimes.
