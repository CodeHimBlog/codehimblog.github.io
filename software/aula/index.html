About Aula

Aula lets you import any study material you already have – PDFs, Word documents, PowerPoint slides, photos of a whiteboard or recordings – and creates a personalized course. The built‑in AI tutor can answer questions using your own content, provide hints on exercises, and generate printable study guides.

The program works with popular AI services such as ChatGPT or Claude, as well as a free local language model that runs offline. After you add your files, Aula automatically organizes them into topics, writes short lessons, creates practice exercises that are checked as you answer, and builds flashcards with spaced‑repetition. You can also generate timed mock exams that match the structure of your real tests and receive a performance report. All generated material can be exported as PDF or Word files for printing or offline review. The app runs on Windows without requiring an account or tracking your activity.

Key features

  • Import PDFs, Word, PowerPoint, images or audio recordings to build a course
  • AI tutor answers questions using your own notes and shows the source page
  • Generates lessons with interactive exercises and instant feedback
  • Creates flashcards with spaced‑repetition for memorisation
  • Builds timed mock exams and provides topic‑wise performance reports
  • Exports study guides, cheat sheets and exam papers as PDF or Word

Frequently asked questions

Is Aula free to use?

Yes, it is completely free and open source.

Is it safe to install?

The installer is signed and the app does not require an online account or tracking.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only if you want to use online AI services like ChatGPT or Claude; a local model works offline.

How do I update the program?

How do I remove Aula?
