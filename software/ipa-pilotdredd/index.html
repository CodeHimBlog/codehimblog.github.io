About IPA PILOTDREDD

IPA PILOTDREDD lets you view all apps you have bought or downloaded from the App Store, see which ones are on your iPhone, and install or update them with a single click. It works by connecting your iPhone via cable or Wi‑Fi and comparing the store library with the device.

The software connects to your Apple ID, retrieves the complete list of purchases since 2008, and displays each app with its icon, developer, version, size and purchase date. Four tabs let you filter the list to show all apps, those not on the iPhone, those already installed, and those already downloaded. When you connect an iPhone, the program shows the status of each app on the device – missing, installed, outdated, or requiring a newer iOS version. You can select one or many apps and install, update, or delete them on the iPhone with a single button. The interface also shows the iPhone model, iOS version, connection type and free space. All actions are performed through the official Apple services; the program does not bypass any protection or share other users' apps.

Key features

  • View the full history of your App Store purchases with icons and details.
  • See at a glance which apps are missing, installed, or need an update on your iPhone.
  • Install or update selected apps on the iPhone with one click.
  • Connect the iPhone via cable or Wi‑Fi and the program automatically detects its status.
  • Filter and search the purchase list, including a rare‑item filter.
  • Batch install, update or delete multiple apps at once.
  • Shows honest status messages such as “Not in App Store” or download errors.
  • Simple interface with separate tabs for all, not on iPhone, on iPhone, and downloaded apps.

Is IPA PILOTDREDD free to use?

The program only accesses your own purchase data and does not bypass Apple security.

How do I update the apps on my iPhone?
